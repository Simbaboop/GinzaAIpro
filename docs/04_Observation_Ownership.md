# Observation Ownership Specification v0.1

## Purpose

Ownership defines who is responsible for reviewing, acting on, or monitoring an observation.

Without ownership, observations become passive records.

With ownership, observations become accountable operational signals.

---

## Core Principle

Every important observation should eventually have an owner.

Ownership connects Governance to Execution.

---

## Definition

An Owner is the person, role, team, or future agent responsible for moving an observation forward.

Owners may include:

- Simba
- Operator
- Builder
- Client
- Sales
- Operations
- Finance
- Governance
- AI Agent

---

## Ownership Responsibilities

An owner may be responsible for:

- review
- investigation
- follow-up
- approval
- task creation
- resolution
- monitoring
- escalation

---

## MVP Owner Field

The MVP will use a simple text field:

```text
owner: string
```
