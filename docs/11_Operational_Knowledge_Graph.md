# Operational Knowledge Graph

## Canonical Specification v1.0

---

# Purpose

The Operational Knowledge Graph (OKG) is the canonical representation of organizational reality inside GinzaAIpro.

It is not a database.

It is not a visualization.

It is not a reporting model.

It is the operational substrate from which intelligence, governance, execution, and learning emerge.

Every first-class operational object becomes part of the graph.

---

# Governing Principle

Organizations are not collections of records.

Organizations are networks of operational relationships.

Operational intelligence therefore emerges from relationships, not isolated data.

---

# Constitutional Law

Capture reality before interpreting reality.

Interpret reality before governing reality.

Govern reality before executing action.

Execute action before measuring outcomes.

Measure outcomes before learning.

Learning continuously improves future interpretation.

---

# Identity

Every node inherits OperationalEntity.

Minimum identity includes:

- id
- traceId
- tenantId
- version
- createdAt
- updatedAt

Identity persists independently of implementation technology.

---

# Graph Invariants

The graph obeys the following rules.

## Reality precedes interpretation.

Events may exist without Observations.

Observations may never exist without Events.

---

## Interpretation precedes governance.

Decisions require at least one Observation.

---

## Governance precedes execution.

Execution always references a Decision.

---

## Execution precedes outcomes.

Outcomes always reference Execution.

---

## Learning references evidence.

Operational Memory references historical Outcomes.

Memory never invents evidence.
