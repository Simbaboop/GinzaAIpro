# Purpose

This document defines how operational information flows through GinzaAIpro.

The information flow is the backbone of the platform.

Every capability should participate in this flow rather than inventing its own lifecycle.

# Stage Responsibilities

## Reality

The external world.

Examples:

- customer calls
- invoices
- appointments
- employees
- equipment
- payments
- AI interactions

---

## Capture

Transforms reality into observable information.

Capture must preserve sufficient context for future reasoning.

---

## Operational Evidence

Stores factual artifacts.

Evidence must exist before interpretation.

Evidence is attributable, timestamped, and auditable.

---

## Runtime Events

Describe what happened inside the platform.

Events are factual.

Events do not determine operational importance.

---

## Operational Signals

Represent operational significance.

Signals filter, prioritize, and route information for downstream consumers.

---

## Operational Memory

Preserves organizational operational history.

Memory accumulates observations, assessments, decisions, executions, and outcomes.

---

## Operational Health

Measures current operational condition.

Health summarizes.

Health does not diagnose.

---

## Operational Cognition

Reasons about evidence, memory, signals, and health.

Produces:

- assessments
- diagnostics
- recommendations

---

## Operational Orchestration

Coordinates multiple operational capabilities.

Determines execution sequencing.

Does not authorize action.

---

## Governance

Evaluates recommendations.

Applies policy.

Approves or rejects execution.

---

## Execution

Performs approved operational actions.

Execution is observable.

Execution generates additional evidence.

---

## Verification

Confirms execution outcomes.

Measures success.

Identifies unexpected consequences.

---

## Learning

Updates organizational knowledge.

Improves future assessments and recommendations.

Learning completes the operational lifecycle.

---

# Information Principles

Information should become:

Evidence

↓

Knowledge

↓

Understanding

↓

Decision

↓

Action

↓

Learning

Never bypass evidence.

Never bypass governance.

Always preserve traceability.
