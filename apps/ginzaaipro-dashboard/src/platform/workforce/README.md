# Workforce Platform

## Purpose

The Workforce Platform provides a unified abstraction for work performed by humans, digital workers, teams, and future automated systems.

It allows GinzaAIpro to coordinate, prioritize, govern, verify, and learn from work regardless of who or what performs it.

---

## Responsibilities

The Workforce Platform owns:

- worker modeling
- human worker representation
- digital worker representation
- team modeling
- assignment modeling
- workload tracking
- verification support
- workforce coordination

---

## Non-Responsibilities

The Workforce Platform does not own:

- business reasoning
- AI execution
- governance approval
- workflow orchestration
- UI rendering
- payroll
- HR administration

Those belong to Operational Intelligence, AI Platform, Governance, Orchestration, Experience, or external systems.

---

## Worker Principle

A worker may be:

- a human employee
- a contractor
- a digital worker
- an AI agent
- a software automation
- a team

The platform should coordinate work based on capability, availability, authorization, reliability, workload, and verification needs.
