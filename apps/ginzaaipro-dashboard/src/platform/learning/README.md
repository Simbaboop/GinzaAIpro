# Operational Learning

## Purpose

Operational Learning transforms validated operational outcomes into reusable organizational knowledge.

Unlike Memory, which stores what happened, Operational Learning determines what should improve and what knowledge should be retained.

---

## Responsibilities

Operational Learning owns:

- outcome evaluation
- recommendation feedback
- pattern discovery
- confidence improvement
- organizational learning
- operational playbooks
- reusable knowledge generation

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Operational Learning does not own:

- operational evidence
- knowledge graph storage
- AI execution
- governance approval
- workflow execution

Those belong to other platform subsystems.

---

---

## Canonical Principle

Every validated outcome should improve future operational decisions.

Unvalidated observations remain evidence, not knowledge.

---

## Learning Sources

- Successful recommendations
- Failed recommendations
- Human corrections
- Workflow outcomes
- Customer outcomes
- Operational metrics
- AI evaluation
- Organizational feedback

---

## Learning Targets

Operational Learning continuously improves:

- business recommendations
- operational playbooks
- AI routing policies
- confidence scoring

- workflow optimization
