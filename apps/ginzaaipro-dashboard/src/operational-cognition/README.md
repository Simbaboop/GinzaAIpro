# Operational Cognition

## Purpose

Operational Cognition is the reasoning layer of GinzaAIpro.

It transforms signals, health snapshots, observations, outcomes, and memory into structured assessments.

Operational Cognition answers:

- What does this mean?
- Why did this happen?
- What evidence supports it?
- How confident are we?
- What should be considered next?

---

## Responsibilities

Operational Cognition owns:

- operational assessments
- diagnostic reasoning
- evidence interpretation
- confidence scoring
- causal analysis
- recommendation preparation
- future forecasting and optimization

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Operational Cognition does not own:

- runtime execution
- signal propagation
- health measurement
- governance approval
- direct action execution
- UI rendering

Those belong to Runtime, Operational Nervous System, Operational Health, Governance, Execution, or Experience layers.

---

## Assessment Principle

An assessment must contain:

- a finding
- supporting evidence
- confidence level
- severity
- recommended next consideration

Cognition without evidence is not trusted cognition.
