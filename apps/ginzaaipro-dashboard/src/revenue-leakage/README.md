# Revenue Leakage Intelligence

## Purpose

Revenue Leakage Intelligence identifies where operational breakdowns cause actual or probable revenue loss.

It answers:

- Where is revenue leaking?
- What evidence supports the finding?
- What operational chain caused the leakage?
- How severe is the leakage?
- What should be investigated or corrected next?

---

## Responsibilities

Revenue Leakage owns:

- leakage findings
- leakage severity
- estimated revenue impact
- supporting evidence references
- graph relationship references
- recommended next consideration

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Revenue Leakage does not own:

- accounting
- payment processing
- tax filing
- direct execution
- governance approval
- UI rendering

Those belong to integrations, governance, execution, or experience layers.

---

## Inputs

Revenue Leakage may consume:

- Operational Evidence
- Operational Knowledge Graph relationships
- Operational Memory
- Operational Health snapshots
- Operational Cognition assessments

---

## Outputs

Revenue Leakage produces:

- Revenue leakage findings
- Estimated impact
- Confidence level
- Evidence references
- Recommended next consideration
