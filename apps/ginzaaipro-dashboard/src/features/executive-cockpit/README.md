# Executive Cockpit

## Purpose

The Executive Cockpit is the primary operational workspace for business owners and managers.

It consolidates operational intelligence into a single, actionable view.

Unlike traditional dashboards, the Executive Cockpit is decision-oriented rather than report-oriented.

---

## Responsibilities

The Executive Cockpit presents:

- Business Health
- Revenue Leakage
- Active Recommendations
- Operational Signals
- Active Operational Journeys
- Governance Queue
- AI Supervisor Summary

---

## Non-Responsibilities

The Executive Cockpit does not:

- perform intelligence computation
- execute workflows
- store operational evidence
- enforce governance

It consumes outputs from platform capabilities.

---

## Design Principles

The Executive Cockpit should:

- surface the most important operational information first
- emphasize actions over reports
- explain why recommendations exist
- expose confidence and evidence
- remain uncluttered
- support progressive disclosure
