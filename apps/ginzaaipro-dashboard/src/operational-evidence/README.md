# Operational Evidence

## Purpose

Operational Evidence is the canonical evidence layer of GinzaAIpro.

It preserves the factual artifacts used by Operational Memory, Operational Health, Operational Cognition, Governance, and future AI reasoning.

Operational Evidence answers:

"What evidence supports this conclusion?"

---

## Responsibilities

Operational Evidence owns:

- evidence records
- evidence source metadata
- evidence summaries
- evidence timestamps
- evidence references
- future evidence retrieval

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Operational Evidence does not own:

- interpretation
- diagnosis
- business health scoring
- recommendations
- governance approval
- execution
- UI rendering

Those belong to Operational Cognition, Operational Health, Governance, Execution, or Experience layers.

---

## Core Principle

Evidence precedes assessment.

Evidence precedes recommendation.

Evidence precedes governed execution.

## Evidence Sources

Operational Evidence may originate from:

- manual observations
- runtime events
- operational signals
- customer communications
- CRM entries
- invoices
- payments
- appointments
- documents
- system logs
- AI outputs
- external integrations

---

## Evidence Rules

Operational Evidence should be:

- attributable
- timestamped
- auditable
- queryable
- immutable where practical
- linked to downstream assessments
