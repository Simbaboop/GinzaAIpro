# Business Recommendations Capability Specification

## Status

Canonical

Version: 0.1

---

# Purpose

Business Recommendations converts operational findings into prioritized, evidence-backed next considerations.

It helps GinzaAIpro move from diagnosis to governed action.

---

# Business Questions

Business Recommendations answers:

- What should be considered next?
- Why is this recommendation important?
- What evidence supports it?
- What is the expected impact?
- Does this require governance before execution?

---

# Inputs

Business Recommendations may consume:

- Revenue Leakage findings
- Business Health results
- Operational Cognition assessments
- Operational Evidence
- Operational Knowledge Graph relationships
- Operational Memory

---

# Outputs

Business Recommendations produces:

- recommendation
- priority
- rationale
- expected impact
- evidence references
- governance requirement

---

# Dependencies

Platform dependencies:

✓ Operational Evidence

✓ Operational Knowledge Graph

✓ Operational Memory

✓ Operational Cognition

✓ Operational Health

Future dependencies:

- Governance
- Operational Orchestration
- Execution
- Outcome Verification

---

# Success Criteria

The capability should:

- produce explainable recommendations
- prioritize business impact
- expose supporting evidence
- identify governance requirements
- avoid autonomous execution
- improve through outcome learning

---

# Architectural Principles

Business Recommendations follows:

- Evidence Before Recommendation
- Governance Before Execution
- Explainability
- Capability-Oriented Architecture
- Learn From Outcomes
