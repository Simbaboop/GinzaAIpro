# Business Recommendations

## Purpose

Business Recommendations transforms operational findings into prioritized next considerations.

It answers:

- What should the business consider doing next?
- Why does this recommendation matter?
- What evidence supports it?
- What is the expected business impact?
- What requires governance before execution?

---

## Responsibilities

Business Recommendations owns:

- recommendation creation
- recommendation priority
- recommendation rationale
- supporting evidence references
- expected impact summaries

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Business Recommendations does not own:

- governance approval
- direct execution
- workflow orchestration
- UI rendering
- accounting
- legal or compliance authority

Those belong to Governance, Execution, Operational Orchestration, Experience, or regulated specialists.

---

## Inputs

Business Recommendations may consume:

- Revenue Leakage findings
- Business Health results
- Operational Cognition assessments
- Operational Evidence
- Operational Knowledge Graph relationships
- Operational Memory

---

## Outputs

Business Recommendations produces:

- recommended next consideration
- priority
- rationale
- evidence references
- expected impact
- governance requirement flag
