# Operational Journeys

## Purpose

Operational Journeys model complete business scenarios that exercise multiple GinzaAIpro platform capabilities.

A journey demonstrates how operational information flows through the platform from initial observation to organizational learning.

Operational Journeys are not intelligence engines.

They orchestrate existing capabilities into realistic operational scenarios.

---

## Responsibilities

Operational Journeys own:

- business scenario definitions
- end-to-end operational flows
- capability sequencing
- demonstration scenarios
- platform validation

---

## Non-Responsibilities

Operational Journeys do not own:

- intelligence reasoning
- health computation
- governance decisions
- execution logic
- evidence storage
- graph storage

Those belong to the underlying platform capabilities.

## Principle

A journey validates the platform.

A capability solves one problem.

A journey demonstrates how capabilities work together.
