# Canonical Terminology

The authoritative terminology inventory is [`registers/TERMINOLOGY_REGISTER.md`](registers/TERMINOLOGY_REGISTER.md). Use preferred terms from that register and qualify narrower implementation-local meanings.

The most important distinctions are:

- Events are facts; Signals express operational significance.
- Operational Health measures; Operational Cognition diagnoses and recommends.
- Orchestration prepares and proposes before Governance, then coordinates material execution only inside an approved governance envelope; Governance authorizes; Execution acts.
- Observation is a captured operational aggregate; Operational Evidence is the factual substrate; the Operational Knowledge Graph represents lineage and relationships.
- Engine, Service, Capability, Store, and Router name different responsibilities as defined by canonical naming conventions.
- Human constitutional authority owns purpose, policy change, critical exceptions, irreversible actions, and risk limits; deterministic platform policy exercises only explicitly delegated routine authority.
- Emergency override is exceptional, authorized, scoped, time-bound, justified, and immutably audited—not a bypass of mandatory controls.

Terminology ambiguity that changes responsibility or authority must be entered in the conflict register.
