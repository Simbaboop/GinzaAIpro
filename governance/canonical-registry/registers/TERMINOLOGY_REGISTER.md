# Terminology Register

|---|---|---|---|---|
| TERM-0001 | Operational Evidence | Attributable, timestamped factual artifacts used before interpretation. | Some earlier documents say Observation where later flow inserts Evidence; retain Observation as a captured aggregate, not a synonym for all Evidence. | [`INFORMATION_FLOW.md`](../../../docs/architecture/INFORMATION_FLOW.md) |
| TERM-0002 | Runtime Event | Factual statement of what happened inside the platform. | “Operational Event” is broader in `docs/10`; qualify the source boundary when used. | [`INFORMATION_FLOW.md`](../../../docs/architecture/INFORMATION_FLOW.md); [`10_Operational_Event_Model.md`](../../../docs/10_Operational_Event_Model.md) |
| TERM-0003 | Operational Signal | Evaluated operational significance used for routing/prioritization. | Do not use Event and Signal interchangeably. | [`ADR-0002`](../../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-0002-Signal-Driven-Architecture.md) |
| TERM-0004 | Operational Orchestration | Before governance: prepares, sequences, simulates, and proposes work. After approval: coordinates material execution inside the approved governance envelope. | TERM-0005 | Operational Nervous System | Subsystem that transforms runtime events into signals and routes them. | `ONS` is acceptable in prose; full descriptive name is preferred in code/folders. | [`NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md`](../../../docs/architecture/NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md) |
| TERM-0006 | Operational Health | Measurement/summarization of current condition; it does not diagnose. | “Runtime Health” is a narrower implementation-local health scope, not the whole subsystem. | [`SUBSYSTEMS.md`](../../../docs/architecture/SUBSYSTEMS.md); [`operational-health/README.md`](../../../apps/ginzaaipro-dashboard/src/operational-health/README.md) |
| TERM-0007 | Operational Cognition | Reasoning over evidence, signals, memory, and health to produce assessments/diagnostics/recommendations. | “Operational Intelligence” is the broader platform capability/flow; avoid treating it as a single subsystem synonym. | [`SUBSYSTEMS.md`](../../../docs/architecture/SUBSYSTEMS.md) |
| TERM-0008 | Observation | Canonical captured operational aggregate with lifecycle, ownership, governance, and traceability. | Later Event/Evidence/OKG models extend representation; they do not erase Observation. | [`06_Observation_Aggregate.md`](../../../architecture/06_Observation_Aggregate.md) |
| TERM-0009 | Operational Knowledge Graph (OKG) | Graph representation connecting operational facts, interpretations, decisions, execution, outcomes, and lineage. | “Canonical operational model” is scoped to graph representation under CON-0003. | [`11_Operational_Knowledge_Graph.md`](../../../docs/11_Operational_Knowledge_Graph.md) |
| TERM-0010 | Governance | Policy evaluation and authorization/rejection of operational action, including the explicit envelope within which material execution orchestration may proceed. | Do not use orchestration, cognition, or AI as synonyms for governance authority. | [`SUBSYSTEMS.md`](../../../docs/architecture/SUBSYSTEMS.md); DEC-0008 |
| TERM-0011 | Capability | Runtime-registered, composable platform functionality. | Engine computes/reasons; Service exposes/coordinates access; Capability participates in runtime. | [`NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md`](../../../docs/architecture/NAMING_CONVENTIONS.md) |
| TERM-0012 | Execution | Observable performance of an approved action, tracked separately from the decision and verified by evidence. | Earlier Observation fields embed execution state; later specifications model Execution as its own aggregate. | [`09_Execution_Aggregate.md`](../../../docs/09_Execution_Aggregate.md) |
| TERM-0013 | Governance envelope | Explicitly approved scope, constraints, risk limits, duration, and permissions within which material execution orchestration may coordinate an authorized plan. | Approval is not general or permanent authority; work outside the envelope returns to Governance. | DEC-0008 |
| TERM-0014 | Human constitutional authority | Human authority over constitutional purpose, policy changes, critical exceptions, irreversible actions, and risk limits. | Distinct from delegated deterministic platform-policy authority for routine execution. | DEC-0009 |
| TERM-0015 | Emergency override | Exceptional authority that is authorized, narrowly scoped, time-bound, justified, and immutably audited; it cannot bypass mandatory constitutional controls. | Not a general bypass or standing privilege. | DEC-0009 |
| TERM-0016 | Organizational Dynamics | Preferred COSMOS scientific/research term for experimental models of organizational constraints, behavior, and possible states. It is non-canonical and non-executable unless separately approved. | “Business Physics,” `business-physics`, `business-physics-engine`, and `BusinessPhysics` are deprecated as GinzaAIpro platform names. Research maturity labels never confer canonical authority. | [`experimental/README.md`](../../../research/cosmos/organizational-dynamics/experimental/README.md); DEC-0010 |
| TERM-0017 | Operational Runtime | Approved future coordination term for lifecycle activity. Runtime coordinates but does not own business reasoning, Governance, Workforce, Execution, Verification, or Learning. No package currently exists under this name. | “Operational Intelligence Runtime,” `operational-intelligence/runtime`, and `OperationalIntelligenceRuntime` are deprecated. “Operational Intelligence” remains the broader platform outcome, not a runtime owner. | DEC-0010 |
| TERM-0018 | Governance Operating System | Constitutional governance authority and process layer defining how governed work is proposed, reviewed, authorized, verified, released, and evolved. It is materialized through the Platform Constitution, applicable Canonical Governance Standards, and established governance-process artifacts rather than a standalone runtime subsystem. | Canonical Governance Standards are standards within this layer, not a synonym for the entire Governance Operating System or a separate authority above it. No standalone Governance Operating System file currently exists. | [`Platform Constitution`](../../constitution/PLATFORM-CONSTITUTION.md); [`CGS README`](../../standards/README.md) |
| TERM-0019 | OperationalCondition | Immutable canonical artifact describing operational state deterministically derived from released Semantic Facts under a released rule set. It sits between Released Semantic Facts and Operational Leakage and preserves identity, lineage, traceability, and rule-set provenance. | It is descriptive only. It must not be used as a synonym for leakage, severity, priority, recommendation, financial impact, execution decision, or AI reasoning. Capability 003 is verified but not released because no Release Record exists. | [`ADR-0003`](../../adr/ADR-0003-Operational-Conditions.md); [`HCES-0003`](../../hces/HCES-0003-Operational-Conditions.md); DEC-0013 |
| TERM-0020 | OperationalLeakage | Immutable canonical artifact representing an economically or operationally material loss, inefficiency, risk, or unrealized opportunity deterministically derived from admissible same-Organization `OperationalCondition` artifacts under released leakage rules. The domain contract is verified, but no Capability 004 runtime engine or Release Record exists. | [`HCES-0004`](../../hces/HCES-0004-Operational-Leakage.md); [`VVR-0004`](../../vvr/VVR-0004-Operational-Leakage.md); [`ADR-0007`](../../decisions/ADR-0007-Preservation-of-Canonical-Operational-Provenance.md); DEC-0014 |
| TERM-0021 | OperationalLeakagePriority | Immutable canonical artifact representing the governed relative importance of one `OperationalLeakage` under released prioritization policy. It must not be used as a synonym for recommendation, ROI, scheduling, resource allocation, optimization, execution authority, AI assignment, or the released legacy `PriorityProfile`. The domain contract is verified, but no prioritization runtime engine or Release Record exists. | [`HCES-0005`](../../hces/HCES-0005-Priority-Profiles.md); [`VVR-0005`](../../vvr/VVR-0005-Operational-Leakage-Priority.md); [`ADR-0004`](../../adr/ADR-0004-Legacy-PriorityProfile-Compatibility.md); [`ADR-0006`](../../adr/ADR-0006-Priority-Artifact-Completeness.md); DEC-0015 |
| TERM-0022 | OperationalRecommendation | Immutable canonical artifact representing a governed intervention hypothesis deterministically derived from one `OperationalLeakagePriority` under an applicable released `RecommendationRule` version.
