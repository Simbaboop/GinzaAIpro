# Experimental Organizational Dynamics Research

## Status

Experimental. Non-canonical. Non-executable.

This directory preserves early COSMOS research models for describing organizational constraints and possible organizational states. It is not a GinzaAIpro platform subsystem and must not be imported by runtime application code without an explicit Project Canon decision.

## Authority boundaries

- These models are not Operational Evidence. An observed state becomes evidence only through the canonical evidence capture, attribution, and audit process.
- These models are not Governance policy and cannot authorize, reject, or constrain execution by themselves.
- Candidate and validated hypotheses are research maturity labels, not canonical status.
- Simulated, forecast, and target states are projections or proposals, never statements of observed reality.
- Any material action derived from this research requires Governance approval and must remain inside the approved governance envelope.

## Contents

- `organizational-dynamics-model.ts` defines experimental scientific models, maturity, provenance, units, evidence references, tenant scope, and confidence.
- `organizational-state.ts` distinguishes observed, simulated, forecast, and target organizational states.

The former platform names and paths are preserved only in Project Canon history.
