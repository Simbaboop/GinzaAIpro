# ADR-0014: Capability Before Intelligence Doctrine

## Status

Accepted for Sprint 2.3.

## Decision

Operational signals must first pass deterministic evidence qualification before they support intelligence. Intelligence engines must consume canonical Evidence rather than promote unqualified signals implicitly.

## Consequence

Source defects remain attributable to validation diagnostics, and downstream intelligence retains evidence identity and provenance. This sprint does not implement intelligence generation or automatic capability changes.
